# Decision log

- Study identified as PMID 38788590 / DOI 10.1016/j.jhazmat.2024.134606.
- Principal target: all cancer deaths (ICD-10 C00–C97), Brazil's 5,570 municipalities, 2000-01-01 through 2019-12-31; exposure is population-weighted municipality daily PM2.5, with the published principal contrast being a 10 µg/m3 increase in the lag 0–2 mean.
- Published design/model: time-stratified case-crossover. Each death is matched to 3–4 same-weekday dates in the same calendar month and municipality; conditional logistic regression adjusts for a temperature cross-basis, relative humidity, and a public-holiday indicator. Age, sex, socioeconomic status, seasonality, long-term trend and weekday are controlled by matching.
- The publisher PDF endpoint returned HTTP 403 and was not blindly retried, as required for an other-4xx response. A lawful publicly accessible methods reading was used only to recover the study specification; no values, tables, code, or derived data from the authors were used.
- The analysis deliberately made no scientific substitutions. In particular, it did not change the health outcome, use a later period, restrict to a subset of municipalities, use aggregate mortality, or substitute a pollutant/exposure product.
- A request to the current official SIM API included `ano=2019`; its response contained 20 individual records with death dates in 2025. The published OpenAPI specification lists only `limit` and `offset` for this endpoint. Evidence: `raw/sim_api.json`, `raw/swagger.json`, and `raw/sim_api_validation.json`.
- The historical official archive object `DO2000.dbc` was probed under the connection-failure retry schedule. Every completed logged probe has zero bytes and a timeout. Full request evidence is retained in `logs/sim_archive_attempts.jsonl`. Duplicate entries labelled attempt 2/3 arose when the execution wrapper interrupted an in-flight probe and a subsequently issued probe overlapped; they are retained rather than erased. They are infrastructure evidence, not new scientific runs.
- Because the required individual historic outcome records cannot be retrieved, no target-period case/control strata can be formed. It follows that exposure assignment, meteorological covariates, holiday flags, and a conditional logistic fit cannot validly be performed. Header-only exposure/panel files and an explicit non-execution model report are supplied to make this stopping decision auditable.
- Outcome: completed_blocker. No estimate, confidence interval, or attributable count is reported.
