# Decision log — PMID 33484722

- **Target identified from the supplied article.** The study used 36 OECD countries (membership as of March 2020), 2017 population-weighted annual PM2.5, and national confirmed-case and death rates at days 60 and 80 after each country's first reported case. The article fit OLS models to log rates with age 65+, population density, GDP, physicians, hospital beds, PM2.5 concentration, and PM2.5 exposure above the WHO guideline.
- **Principal reconstructable comparison.** This reanalysis fit the stated log-rate OLS structure for PM2.5 at all four declared outcome/time comparisons (case and death rates, days 60 and 80). The primary comparison is case rate on day 80, selected because the paper reported all four models but did not nominate one. Rates are per 10,000; the response is `log(rate)`. The reported outcome ratio is `exp(beta_PM2.5)` for a +1 microgram/m3 contrast. This is an outcome ratio, not an odds ratio or risk ratio.
- **Exposure.** To comply with the independent-source requirement, no author pollution data or World Bank PM2.5 value was downloaded. The exposure table is the 2017 country-level population-weighted PM2.5 measure from SatPM/Atmospheric Composition Analysis Group, distributed by OWID. Its metadata and checksum are preserved in the source manifest.
- **Outcomes.** The paper used WHO situation reports from January 21–June 7, 2020. The reanalysis uses the public OWID historical COVID country series. For each country, day zero is its first date with `total_cases >= 1`; totals on calendar day +60 and +80 are divided by 2019 World Bank population. The difference is a source and historical-revision substitution, not a retry.
- **Covariates.** World Bank API data were retrieved for the paper's non-pollution covariates. `covariate_sanitization.json` documents the mandatory exclusion rule before data inspection/modeling; this particular covariate-only table contained no forbidden columns. Physician values follow the article's 2017, then 2016, then 2015 fallback. Beds use 2015, 2014, then the larger available 2013/2012 value. GDP is total current USD in 2019, expressed in billions because the original unit was not specified.
- **Coverage.** Exposure and outcome rows exist for all 36 countries at both times. The US has no physician observation in the prescribed World Bank 2015–17 range, producing 35 complete cases at each time. It was not imputed and no alternative physician source was substituted. This is recorded in the blocker ledger and panel.
- **Execution.** Requests succeeded on first attempts. The retrieval log records URL, response status, byte count, delay, and SHA-256. No scientific decision was changed after observing model results.
- **Schema resolution.** The output schema prohibits extra top-level keys. The required fidelity record is included through `result.json.artifacts.fidelity_record`; all model contrasts are in the linked `model_output.csv`.
