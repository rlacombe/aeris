# Autonomous reanalysis architecture

Self-driving AERIS separates scientific meaning from execution choices.

1. The agent explores the complete article and public data routes under a
   study-independent safety envelope.
2. `lock_reanalysis_invariants` protects the question, outcome and population,
   pollutant, principal contrast, broad geography and period, and prohibited
   evidence. Its evidence map proves that the agent searched the complete paper,
   data-availability material, supplements, repositories, covariates, and models.
3. Retrieval routes, stations, coverage subsets, optional covariates, and
   implementation details remain open during exploration.
4. `amend_exposure_contract` records execution choices in append-only versions.
   Every amendment cites saved evidence, explains its scientific basis, labels
   its fidelity impact, and confirms that numerical agreement played no role.
   The first amendment separates outcome, exposure, and analysis support; compares
   candidate plans; and records whether a successful probe can expand.
5. `update_research_notebook` preserves the active objective, evidence,
   hypotheses, routes, decisions, blockers, and next actions across restarts.
6. A terminal stop requires executable attempt records with a command or URL,
   observed response, format inspection, and saved evidence.
7. A fitted result requires unit-level attempt evidence, completed feasible
   expansion, reported-covariate recovery evidence, and all principal variants.

The host may browse public sources, inspect endpoints, write code, install
bounded parsers, and repair failures within the safety envelope. AERIS remains
responsible for exposure design, discovery, assignment, computation or
integration, diagnostics, provenance, decisions, and analysis handoff.

The redesign removes fixed autonomous batch sizes, early source and station
commitments, immutable implementation choices, and checklist-only blocker
claims. Interactive exposure assessment retains its existing approval flow.
